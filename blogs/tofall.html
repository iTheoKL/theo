To Fall

On Icarus, on Daedalus, and on what it costs to make something worth the name.

What is a fall

But the memory of a height

Once touched?

For who is Daedalus,

But the name remembered

Because Icarus fell?

Who are you but an artist,

If you can't finish an art

When the art demands of you?

For I do not seek death.

I seek a life

With an art so great that

It demands everything of me.

And if I fall,

Let the earth remember

How high I once was.

For the point was never

To die.

But to find something

Worth dying for.

— T.K.L.
