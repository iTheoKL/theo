01 — Personnel

Subject profile

FILE STATUS: ACTIVE · AGENT 031

Software is a kind of architecture. First: what must the system be? Then: how should it be built?

The answer is in the fit of its parts, the assumptions at its boundaries, and whether it is honest about what it does. A clean interface matters. Knowing what it hides matters more.

Memory layout, cache behaviour, concurrency, and the cost of moving data hold my attention. C++, Rust, Go, and Python are instruments; none is a substitute for understanding the system.

Outside the terminal: VALORANT, VCT, and music. The round before the shot is often the more interesting one: information withheld, space conceded, a rotation made too early.

04 — The read

What remains

A WORKING PRINCIPLE

The surface is rarely the whole system. Start with the boundary: what enters, what is assumed, and what must still hold when the conditions change.

In a round, the same discipline applies. Information withheld. Space conceded. A rotation made too early. The visible outcome arrives last; the decision was there first.

I.

In systems

  • Make assumptions explicit.
  • Keep the cost of data movement visible.
  • Test the boundary, not only the happy path.

II.

In a round

  • Read the space before taking it.
  • Notice what the opponent has stopped showing.
  • Do not confuse a quiet round with an empty one.

III.

In the work

  • Build for the conditions that actually exist.
  • Prefer a precise explanation to an impressive claim.
  • Leave enough evidence for the next person to verify it.

STANDARD

The claim is not the result. The method, its limits, and the evidence belong beside it.
