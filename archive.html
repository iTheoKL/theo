P—002 / JAVASCRIPT · SECURITY

ThreatLens

A browser extension that examines suspicious links and explains its verdict.

Client-side heuristics distinguish safe, suspicious, and malicious URLs without disguising uncertainty. The result is a verdict with its cause, not a score without an account.

P—003 / PYTHON · HEALTHCARE

MedBridge

A medication-management platform assembled under a hackathon deadline.

Prescription intake, interaction checks, and symptom cross-referencing were brought into one working system. The difficult part was not naming the components; it was deciding precisely what each could expect from the others.

OCR weights were loaded ahead of use to reduce cold-start delay. Session guards and interface changes connected the separate pieces. Integration exposed what isolated demonstrations concealed: an unexpected format, a missing check, a delay in the wrong place.

The lesson was not that coordination becomes unnecessary when one person writes enough code. It was that someone has to keep the whole system in view. Under a deadline, an unowned boundary does not stay harmless for long.

W—001 / VERSE · AUG 2026

To Fall

On Icarus, on Daedalus, and on what it costs to make something worthy of the name.

What is a fall

But the memory of a height

Once touched?

Who is Daedalus,

But a name remembered

Because Icarus fell?

Who are you but an artist,

If you abandon your art

When the art demands everything of you?

For I do not seek death.

I seek a life

With an art so great that

It demands everything of me.

And if I fall,

Let the earth remember

How high I once was.

W—002 / NOTE · NOV 2025

First principles

A result is more convincing when its assumptions are visible.

I am interested in software whose behaviour can be explained: where the cost comes from, which conditions must hold, and what happens when they do not. Speed without that account is an observation, not yet an understanding.

The standard is straightforward. Know what you have built. Know where it stops being correct. Do not mistake an impressive demonstration for an answer to either question.
