Cipher CLI

A command-line tool in Go for encrypting local files. AES-256-GCM authenticates the ciphertext; Argon2id derives the key from a password. The key is never stored.

CIPHER Authenticated encryption AES-256-GCM
KDF Key derivation 64 MB Argon2id
