A Little About the Apprentice

Un regard tourné vers la Terre, l'autre vers l'infini.

I'm a first-year apprentice of the Craft, currently in the phase where small programs and half-finished games teach you more than any lecture ever could. Hangman one week, a bit of ML the next. The early stuff. It's a lot of not knowing what you're doing and figuring it out anyway, which turns out to be most of what this work really is.

The destination, though, is clear: Cloud Security Architecture. I got there by laying the options out honestly. Quant Dev, Software Dev, Cloud Sec. All worthy paths. I chose Cloud Sec because it was the most systems-oriented of the three, and that's the kind of thinking I'm drawn to. It's a long road from here, but having a direction makes the early study feel like it's going somewhere.

Outside the terminal, I play video games (Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 and Cyberpunk 2077 are firm favourites), I follow Valorant esports more closely than I probably should, and I fly the EDG banner without shame. I listen to a great deal of music and lose an unreasonable number of hours to YouTube, which by now I've simply accepted as part of who I am. Still figuring most things out. That's rather the point.
