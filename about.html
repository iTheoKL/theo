About

A little about me

I build things for the web and beyond — with a deep interest in systems that are elegant, resilient, and quietly powerful.

Based somewhere between a terminal window and a cup of coffee, I've spent years working across distributed systems, developer tooling, and the occasional side project that refuses to stay small. I believe good software should be invisible in the best way: present when needed, out of the way when not.

My approach to engineering leans towards simplicity — not because complexity is bad, but because clarity is hard and worth chasing. I write a lot of Go and Rust, think carefully about APIs, and have opinions about log levels.

Outside of code, I'm drawn to language, philosophy, and the overlap between the two. The quote on the homepage is a reminder that some of the most real things are the ones we build in our minds first.

I'm currently open to interesting conversations, collaborations, and problems worth solving.
