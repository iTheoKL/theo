About

I ask what a system must be before deciding how to build it.

The interesting work happens at the boundaries: between components, between an abstraction and the machine beneath it, between what passes a test and what remains correct under pressure. I prefer decisions I can account for.

That is what draws me to quantitative development, and to firms such as Jane Street. Precision has consequences there. I want to work where the details cannot be delegated to luck.

Below the abstraction

Memory layout, cache behaviour, concurrency, and the cost of moving data interest me more than the number of tools in a stack. A clean interface is useful. Understanding what it hides is necessary.

Outside the terminal

VALORANT and VCT hold more of my attention than any other game. I follow EDG. A round is often decided before the shot: information withheld, space conceded, a rotation made too early.

I play Fade, Clove, and Sova most often. Chamber has had more time this act; Vyse remains a preference when the composition allows it. More often than not, I fill what four other players have left.

Viper occupies a different place. She is my favourite agent, perhaps my favourite fictional character. Her utility asks for patience and coordination. Ranked seldom supplies both. I still know how I want to play her.
